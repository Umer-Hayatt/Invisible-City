## Scope

Home page (`app/page.tsx`), mode: Persuade. The world it sets (tokens, type, panels, buttons) carries into every other route; those are Operate surfaces and take it with restraint.

Audience: hackathon judges deciding in minutes; Citizens on phones. Action: Report a problem. Proof: live counts per stage and per sector, and a real Fix confirmed by a Citizen (before and after photos).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Islamabad's own road signage becomes the record of what got fixed; the home page is one gantry sign over the city, not a SaaS hero with a screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Sign green (#00653a) owns whole panels; white reflective lettering and a white inset border on every panel; sign yellow only for warnings and the exit tab; asphalt ground at night (dark mode), pale concrete by day. Overpass (a highway-gothic descendant) for all type, Overpass Mono only for figures. Lucide arrows as route arrows.

STORY: The visitor reads "Fixed means a citizen said so", sees which sectors are neglected right now, follows the route line Reported → Assigned → Being fixed → After-photo → Confirmed with live counts, sees one real confirmed Fix, and taps Report a problem.
